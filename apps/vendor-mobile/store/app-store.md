## Description
Dilivygo Vendor lets restaurant and shop owners manage incoming Dilivygo orders right from a phone or tablet.

KEY FEATURES
- New-order push notifications even when the app is closed.
- Accept, prepare, and mark ready in one tap.
- See every active delivery on a single screen with live status.
- Chat with the customer or the assigned rider directly.
- Quickly mark items 86'd / out-of-stock without opening a laptop.
- Switch between multiple shops in your workspace.

BUILT FOR THE FRONT-OF-HOUSE
Designed for the rush. Big touch targets, persistent ringer for new orders, and an "always-on" display option keep the kitchen in sync with the queue.

PRIVACY & DATA
The app only shows orders for the shops you are authorised to manage. Your account is scoped by your workspace admin — there is no public sign-up.
